First public release of Miga.
• Recipe books with photos, sub-recipes, adjustable servings and a step-by-step cooking mode with timers and voice control.
• Shopping list sorted by category and supermarket aisle, with several lists, templates, dictation and a product scanner (Open Food Facts).
• Import recipes from photos or web pages with AI, using your own key.
• Backups, recipe packs and syncing with your own server.
• Available in English and Spanish.
